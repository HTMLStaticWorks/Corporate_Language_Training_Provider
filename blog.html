Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our programs and methodology.

How are language levels certified?

All our courses align with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). Levels A1 through C2 are assessed through official pre-tests and certified exit examinations reviewed by our qualified native instructors.

Can we customize the business vocabulary for our industry?

Yes, our Enterprise and Business plans allow companies to co-design custom learning modules focused on specific vocabulary relevant to fields like finance, IT, manufacturing, and law.

What metrics are tracked on the HR Manager Dashboard?

L&D coordinators can track employee login frequency, lesson completion rates, real-time CEFR level progression, and assessment scores. Automated reporting is available in PDF and CSV formats.
